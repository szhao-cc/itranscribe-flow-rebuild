# Growth Audit — iTranscribe

**Live:** https://szhao-cc.github.io/itranscribe-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic iTranscribe already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in iTranscribe's own design. Prepared by Adapty, November 2026.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give people who close the paywall one more option | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Name their use in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Read their answers back before the price | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Put your 4.6 and the press quotes next to the decision | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Lead with the year, and say "Activated" after the purchase | ARPU +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for iTranscribe. Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from iTranscribe's own App Store listing and in-app screens.

## Run it locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000/. Add `?open=proto` to land straight on the prototype.
